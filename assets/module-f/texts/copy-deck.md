## How it works

**Page title:** How SwapLoop works — swap or charge  
**Meta description:** Learn how SwapLoop battery swaps and monitored e-bike charging bays work for compatible e-bikes in Shanghai.

**Intro:** SwapLoop does not force every rider onto one model. Compatible removable batteries are exchanged at a cabinet; e-bikes with integrated batteries use a monitored charging bay.

**Steps:**

1. **Find a station** — choose a `SWAP`, `CHARGING`, or `HYBRID` location that matches your bike.  
2. **Reserve** — hold a Battery Slot or E-bike Charging Bay in the SwapLoop rider app.  
3. **Complete safely** — swap a compatible ready pack, or leave the bike to charge and collect when done.

**Comparison:**

| Route | Who it is for | Compatibility | What happens |
| ----- | ------------- | ------------- | ------------ |
| Battery swap | Delivery riders and private owners with compatible removable batteries | `SL-48`, `SL-60` | Exchange at a Battery Swap Cabinet |
| E-bike charging | Owners with integrated batteries | `GB-AC-48`, `GB-AC-60` | Reserve an E-bike Charging Bay, collect when finished |

**Safety note:** Packs are assessed before they return to circulation. Charging bays are monitored. Priority access for partners never bypasses compatibility or safety checks.

**Rider app line:** Live availability and reservations are in the SwapLoop rider app.

**Meta description:** Partner starter and Partner fleet plans for delivery companies, with quotas, overage rates, and priority time windows.

**Intro:** Delivery partners co-fund coverage and can take Partner plans with quota, overage, and optional priority windows at busy stations.

### Partner starter

- Monthly base: **¥2,000**  
- Included uses: **150** / month  
- Overage: **¥6** / use beyond quota  

**Overage discounts (starter):** 0–199 uses → 0%; 200–399 → 5%; 400+ → 15% (discount applies to overage only).

### Partner fleet

- Monthly base: **¥5,000**  
- Included uses: **400** / month  
- Overage: **¥5** / use beyond quota  

**Overage discounts (fleet):** 0–499 → 0%; 500–999 → 10%; 1000+ → 20% (discount applies to overage only).

**Funding line:** A fixed district safety grant helps start stations; partner co-funding and subscriptions reduce long-term grant dependence. This is not a tax and not a personal or business score.
